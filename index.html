First, an apology
Dear Rakeb,

I owe you a birthday wish — and an apology for being late.

I'm sorry I missed the day itself. I didn't want to send just another ordinary birthday message; I wanted to make you something a little different, with some thought behind it.

I still remember the first day in class. You were sitting on the left side, and somehow that little moment stayed in my memory. Maybe it was one of those ordinary moments that you don't realize you'll remember later.

And honestly, one of the first things I noticed about you was your eyes. There's something genuinely beautiful about them. They have a way of catching attention without even trying. It's difficult to explain, but they're the kind of detail that makes someone memorable.

We only got to know each other this year, in Grade 11, Section B, but I'm glad our paths crossed. Sometimes the people we meet in a new school year make it a little more memorable.

It took me longer than I planned, so this isn't an excuse. It's simply my way of saying your birthday deserved a little extra thought.

I hope you can forgive the delay and accept this late wish with the good intention it comes with.

With warm wishes,

Someone who wanted to make you smile.
